A small town in the Netherlands has a new way to keep people safe on the road. In the town of Bodegraven, traffic lights now shine on the ground, not only in the air. The town put long strips of light along the edge of the sidewalk at one busy crossing. When the light turns red, a bright red line appears on the ground. When it turns green, the line turns green too. The idea is simple. Many people look down at their phones while they walk, and they do not look up at the traffic light above them. So the town moved the light to the place where people already look, the ground under their feet. Town leaders say the strips are cheap to build and easy to fix. Other towns in the country are now watching to see if they work.
