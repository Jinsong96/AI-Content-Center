A New Year's Promise

It was New Year's Eve. Quinn was excited. In just two hours, it was going to be a new year!

The year would be 2018 at midnight. Quinn was going to make a New Year's resolution. A resolution is a promise to do something to make yourself better.

Quinn promised to practise speaking clearly and loudly because he would like to give a speech in front of many people.

It was hard to keep a resolution, but Quinn was going to try very hard to keep it till the end of the year!
