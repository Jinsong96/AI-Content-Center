The farmer pays one dollar to use the donkey for the day. It is a very hot day. The two men want to stand in the shade cast by the donkey's body.

There is a problem. The shadow is only big enough to protect one of them from the hot sun. The two men begin to argue.

" paid for the donkey for one day." cries one farmer. "The shadow is mine to use today." The man who owns the donkey says, "I let you use my donkey. But I did not rent its shadow to you."
