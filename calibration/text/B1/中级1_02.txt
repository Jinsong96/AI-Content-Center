Insect Names

Insects are funny little creatures. Some of them have interesting names like ladybug, grasshopper and butterfly. Are all ladybugs ladies? Does a grasshopper really hop on grass? Is a butterfly really butter that flies? Some ladybugs are girls and some are boys. A grasshopper really does like to hop on grass. And of course butter cannot fly, but butterflies can.

Not all ladybugs are ladies!
