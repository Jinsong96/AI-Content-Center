Outside our kitchen window, there is a tree. No one in our family knew what kind of tree it was when we first moved in.

Early that spring, the tree started to grow little buds and yellow petals. But there was still snow on the ground! At first, I thought the yellow buds were the leaves of the tree. My sister agreed and guessed, "It must be the cold weather that made the tree leaves yellow, instead of green." But a few weeks later, green leaves began to appear on the tree branches along with the yellow petals! We then realized that the yellow "leaves" that appeared first were actually flowers!

This was the first time we saw a tree bear flowers
