Fire is dangerous. House fires kill many people. But there is something you can do about them. Get a smoke detector. Smoke detectors save lives. How? They tell you to get out of a burning house.

Many house fires happen at night when people are sleeping. A fire starts slowly. First there is smoke... a lot of smoke. It's actually the smoke that kills most people, not the fire itself.

A smoke detector gives you an early warning about a fire. It makes a very loud noise when there is smoke. It wakes you up so you can get out of the house. That's how smoke detectors work.
