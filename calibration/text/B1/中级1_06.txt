The Colour of Food

My mom likes to teach me how to eat healthy food. It's very simple. She says, "Pay attention to the colour of the food on the table, If it's colourful, you have a healthy meal. If it's just one colour, that's not good."

Ilook at the food on the table and think, "Mom is right. Vegetables are colourful. Meat is not."

So we should eat more vegetables and only a little meat.

Pass the beans, please!
