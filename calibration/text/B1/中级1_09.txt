Special Places

My uncle has travelled all over the world. He has visited the seven wonders of the world.

One day, I asked him, "Uncle, what is the most special place you have ever visited in all the world?"

"That's easy." he replied. "Once when I was a young boy like you, my mother took me to the library. She showed me how to borrow a book. I will never forget sitting in that library with my mother!"

Then he said, "To this day, a library is the most special place in all the world."
