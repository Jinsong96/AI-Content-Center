Athletes of the World

Around the world, there are many different cultures, beliefs, and ways of life. But there is something that all countries have in common. They all have athletes. Athletes are similar in many ways. They all train, which builds strength, speed, and endurance. They work hard to improve their skills. All athletes, in their own way, work hard, have fun, and try to win. Athletes are different from one another, too. Some are young and some are old. Some are boys and some are girls. Some athletes run, some jump, some lift, some pull, some catch, and some throw. Some glide, some hit, some block, some shoot, some score, some win, and some lose. Some athletes are thin, like cross-country runners. Others have large bulky muscles, like weightlifters. Some athletes are very tall. Think of basketball players. Others are tiny. Gymnasts are perhaps the smallest of all.

Some athletes compete individually. Some perform in pairs like figure skaters. Hockey players always compete on a team. Sometimes athletes, like sailors, use equipment. Other athletes, like swimmers, use none. Some athletes compete on land. Think of a bicycle race! Others compete on the water. Think of rowers! Still others compete on a special court. Think of tennis! Some athletes are professionals - they make money for doing their sport. NBA basketball players are a good example. Other athletes are amateur athletes. That means that they make little or no money to train and compete.
