The Science Fair

Mariam and Jasmine are members of the Science Club. They re keen to learn about volcanoes - why they erupt, and how they may affect humans. When their school organizes the annual science fair, Mariam and Jasmine are eager to set up their project and give a presentation about volcanoes. Mariam pins up a poster.
