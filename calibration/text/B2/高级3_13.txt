Ollie's Echo

The class was studying sound waves. The kids said the sound of Ollie's voice made really big waves. Ollie simply had a big voice. "Use your inside voice please, Ollie," her teacher said. Ollie tried, but her voice was just too big for inside. When she raised her hand in class, the kids covered their ears. But they stopped doing that on the day of their field trip. The class hiked up a mountain trail and stopped at 'Echo Point. Miss Wong said, "This is called Echo Point because when we speak, our sounds bounce off that mountain and echo back.
