Energy

Energy - a powerful word. What is energy, anyway? In scientific terms, energy is the ability to do work. There are many different kinds of energy. But energy can change from one form to another. You have energy too. Energy is what keeps your body going every day. Because of your energy, you can walk, run, jump, skip, sing, do math problems, think, and do everything else. You get your energy from the food you eat and from sleeping. There's another kind of energy in your home and school. That kind of energy powers our TVs, phones, games, refrigerators, stoves, heaters, air conditioners, lights, vehicles, and more. Most of the energy powering those things comes from natural resources in the earth, for example gas and oil. Our natural resources have a limited supply. Once they're used up, we won't have them any more. So we need to conserve or save our natural resources.

You might think saving energy is an adult problem and it is. Adults are working on making things use less energy, like cars, appliances, houses, and everything we use. And they're working to find renewable energy sources. But guess what? Kids can make a difference too! You can use less energy by starting some simple habits. Here are some energy saving habits you can try today. * Turn off lights when you don't need them. * Watch less TV and go outside and play. * Unplug chargers from the wall when not in use. * Open your windows instead of turning on the air conditioning. * Don't let the water run while you're brushing your teeth. Let's all make some simple changes and conserve our planet's natural resources. The future will thank us!
