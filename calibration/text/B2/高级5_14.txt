Bells and Clocks and Time

Every school has an electric bell. It rings when school starts. It rings at recess. How do you know it's time to go home? The bell rings. It was different in the past. Teachers rang a hand bell. They let students go home when everyone got hungry. For most of history, people did not worry much about the time. Nobody had a watch or a cellphone. A person simply looked at the sun in the sky. In the past, people did not have alarm clocks. Instead, they woke when the sun rose. They went to sleep when it set. People wanted a better system. They put big bells in town halls and church steeples. People rang these bells by hand. It was hard because the bells were so big and heavy. Everyone in town could hear the bells. The bells told them when it was time to worship, time to go to work, and time for dinner. Clocks were invented over 600 years ago. Clocks are machines.

Eventually, watchmakers began making small clocks. People could have clocks in their homes. Watchmakers began making watches. These are tiny clocks you wear on your wrist or in your pocket. Everyone set their watches and clocks to match the time on the town clock. Instead of meeting a friend about midday, you could meet a friend at exactly 3:11 p. m. Today, most people use digital watches or clocks, which run on electricity or batteries. People don't need to match their time to the town clock anymore. All they have to do is align their clock or watch with the time displayed on the Internet, a computer, or even a cellphone. Don't be late!
