Master of Disguise

Splash! I'm swimming as fast as I can, but the humans are catching up. I had better find a place to hide. Luckily, I'm a Mimic Octopus. I sneak between some pieces of coral reef and focus on my body. Then, I change colour! Are you surprised? Let me explain. A squid, an octopus, or a cuttlefish has this strange ability: to change colour in a matter of seconds! With thousands and thousands of special cells right under our skin, underwater creatures like me can blend into our surroundings to hide from predators. Wait, shh! Here come the humans. Will they see me? No, they swam right by me! Thanks to my special cells, 1 look just like a coral reef. And, as an octopus, I have an extra neat way to make myself invisible. My skin has these little projections that can actually change texture to help me disguise myself better,

Look, they're spiky! And watch me change them into small bumps. Blue, red, brown, yellow.. Any colour I need to stay safe. Can I come out now? No, not yet... because, here comes a damselfish! I sneak further into a hole, leaving two arms out, and change their colour to black and white. Now, that pesky damselfish will think I'm a sea snake and swim away. Sea snakes love to hunt damselfish. Ibet you didn't know an octopus could be so smart! Well, watch out! You never know if I'm watching you.
