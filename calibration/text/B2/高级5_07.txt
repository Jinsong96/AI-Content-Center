The Beating Heart

On a sunny winter day, Jolie and Hong take their sleds to the big hill. They climb to the top and slide down to the bottom. They do it over and over again. On one trip to the top, Jolie says, "My heart pounds really fast when we climb the hill. But not when we slide down. That's weird. It s way more exciting to slide down the hill!" "Mine beats faster when climbing the hill, too. I think I know why" says Hong: Jolie sits down to catch her breath. Her heart is still beating fast."Why?" "See my fist?" Hong holds up her fist. "Yup." "Your heart is a muscle about as big as this fist." Jolie makes a fist, too. "Hmmm, right now my heart feels bigger than that." 'Your heart pumps blood to your body. The blood brings your body oxygen and nutrients" says Hong. "When you exercise, you

need more oxygen. So your heart pumps faster to get your body what it needs." Jolie feels her chest. "Mine's still pounding." "Mine too" explains Hong. 1 "'So I guess when we're climbing the hill, our bodies need more oxygen, which makes our hearts Pump faster." "That makes sense" says Jolie. "When we're sliding down the hill, we' re just riding the sleds, so our hearts don't need to work as hard." Hong and Jolie make snow angels while they rest. "Let's check our heart rates now," says Hong. They check their heart rates by feeling the blood pulse in the artery in their necks. "My heart beat is back to normal," says Jolie. "Mine too" adds Hong. "Let's slide some more" grins Jolie.
