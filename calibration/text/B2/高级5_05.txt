Why Do Cats Land on Their Feet?

Shiva is Kara's cat. She loves attention. She always climbs on Kara while she plays, while she watches television, and even while she eats! Sometimes, the cat climbs onto the furniture, even if it's very high up. There is one place she is not allowed: the table. One day, while Kara does her homework, the cat jumps onto the table. "No! Bad kitty!" yells Kara. The cat is frightened by Kara's yelling. She falls off the table. Somehow, though, she lands on her feet- her four paws. Kara wonders how cats always land on their feet, no matter how big the jump. She asks her mom. Kara's mom explains, "Cats have a good sense of balance. So they know where the ground is even when they are upside down in the air. Their spines are more flexible than a human's. This means they can twist their bodies and adjust their position in mid-air, so however they're falling, they can land on their feet."

Kara picks up her cat, which immediately starts to purr. "1 guess she forgives me for yeling" grins Kara. "But what if a cat falls from really high up? Like my bedroom window?" "Well" says her mom, thinking. "A cat is smaller and has lighter bones than a human, and has lots of fur. Some cats even spread themselves out like a parachute to slow down their fall! The higher up they are, the more time they have in the air to adjust their position. They will still land on their feet." Kara and Shiva look at the house's windows. "But I don't want either of you jumping out to test that, even in the name of science!" winks Kara's mom.
