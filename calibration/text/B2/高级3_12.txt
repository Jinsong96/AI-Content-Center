Cars

It was about 1672. A European man from Belgium was visiting a Chinese emperor. As a gift to the Emperor, the man made a toy. The toy was powered by steam. It had wheels. It was about 65 centimetres long. This toy might have been the very first modelcar.

Of course, the car was too small for a person. But, many people think that this was the beginning of trains, cars and even steam-powered boats. Today, cars are everywhere. We all know that they pollute a lot. They also crowd our roads. And they can be very unsafe if people aren't careful when they drive them. But why do people still like cars? Because cars are good in a lot of ways too. Cars help people get to places that are far away. They travel

long distances quickly. And, they bring families together. Of course, most people in the world don't have a car. Some people have never even seen a car. Maybe this isn't such a bad thing. Can you imagine how much fun people would have if they drove horse carriages, or if the streets were full of people riding bikes? You could chat with the person next to you while riding your bike. One hundred years ago, there were only a few cars in the whole world. Today, there are over a billion cars on our little planet. Think about how much gas these cars can burn!
