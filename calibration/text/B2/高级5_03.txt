A Dangerous Wind

Sai and his family are worried. The news predicted a tornado would pass right through their neighbourhood. Hundreds of people are evacuating with their pets and a few belongings. Like many others, Sai's family is Leaving their home in their car, heading for the shelters. "Why do we have to go?" asks Sai. He doesn't want to leave his home and his toys. "Tornadoes are one of the most violent and powerful types of weather. They can pull a tree out of the ground, or lift up a cow and drop it hundreds of metres away."' says his mom. "What matters most is that we are safe. The stuff can always be replaced if the tornado damages our home" says his dad. "Why is the tornado angry?" Sai asks. Tears escape his

eyes. He doesn't want the tornado to lift up the roof of their house and take his toys. "Sometimes, during a thunderstorm in a cloud, the winds start to spin together, forming a funnel-shaped cloud. When they sweep up air from the ground, the wind funnel gets bigger. It isn't angry. Its winds are going too fastl" answers Mom. "How fast?" Sai stammers. "Anywhere from 100 to 300 kilometres per hour!" says Dad. "All we can do now is hope it is a small tornado and pray for our community." Mom nods. 'Is there anything we can do about it?" Sai asks. "Unfortunately, nature can. be dangerous. Floods and tornadoes can hurt a lot of people. Thats why we have to be prepared."
