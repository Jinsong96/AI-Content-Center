Fave you ever sat outside on a dark evening and looked up at the stars? They appear very close, but actually they are very far away. Imagine living 10,000 years ago as cavemen did. Imagine seeing a shooting star, a meteorite, or an eclipse of the sun! These things must have frightened people a long time ago. It's good that science has explained what these things mean. We don't have to be scared anymore. But it's important to realize that there is still a lot that we don't understand about the universe. We know today that some stars are so far away that you could not reach them even in a billion years. And some of those lights in the sky are quite close - only 60 or 70 million kilometres away.
