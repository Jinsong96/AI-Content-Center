Look at a Leaf

It was a beautiful autumn afternoon. Stephan and his dad sat under a tree and counted the leaves as they fell. First, they counted one by one, then two by two and three by three. Then a gust of wind came up, the branches shook, and a flurry of leaves flew off. They tossed about - too many to count - a burst of orange, red, and brown. Leaves scattered and swirled in the air, then touched down on the boy. A pile of leaves grew up so high that they covered him from head to toe. The boy jumped up, gasped for breath, and kicked the leaves away. "1 couldn't breathe in there " he said. His dad looked on and simply said, "Did you know that leaves breathe too?" He picked a green leaf from the tree and handed it to his son. The boy was puzzled. That couldn't be true. He picked up a green leaf and studied it closely. "How do they breathe?'"' he asked "Come. Let's have a look."

The father went inside the house. He took out a magnifying glass and said, "We'll need to look closely." Stephan looked very closely at the leaf. "Dad, I just can't see how leaves breathe." "Here.
