Under the Burning Sun

Sorin and his family are on a camping trip. They drove two hours north to arrive at a camping site. They are going to stay there for a week, sleeping in a tent, swimming in the lake, eating barbecue, walking in the woods, and having lots of fun. It's the family's favourite summertime vacation. The first day, they all wake up early. The parents are taking a hike in the woods. Sorin and his brother go straight to the water. They dive off a big rock with a splash and swim around. They spend the whole morning on the beach. Every time they get too hot, the sparkling lake keeps them cool. Sorin sees another family swimming off a dock. They all have shirts on. That is so strange, thinks Sorin. Why would someone wear a shirt while they swim? The morning passes quickly. By the time they walk back to their tent, Sorin's back and shoulders feel a little... sore. A few hours later, Sorin's back feels really sore. "Dad," he says."It hurts!"

His father has a look. He gasps. "You have a sunburn!" he says. "Your back is very red. And it looks sore. Look at your arms!" "Sorry, sweetie"' says his mother. "We should have put more sunblock on you. It must have washed off in the lake!" "Ouch!" Sorin shrinks as he puts his shirt back on. "We can soothe your skin with some lotion," says his mother. 'Tomorrow, we'll put on more sunscreen and renew it every few hours!" says Sorin's father. "So we will better protect ourselves from the sun." "How does that work?" asks Sorin. "'Sunscreen contains ingredients that keep dangerous UV rays from getting to your skin" explains Sorin's mother. Sorin nods. "1 think IIl wear a shirt" he says. "Even when I'm swimming!"
