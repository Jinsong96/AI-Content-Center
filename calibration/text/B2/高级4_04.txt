It's All in the Family

Phillip lives with his parents in the west end of the city. He is proud of having a large extended family- aunts, uncles, and cousins, all living nearby. One day, he and his dad baked a cake for his mom's surprise birthday party. They followed a recipe and the cake smelled delicious. They finished decorating it just as the relatives began to arrive. As his relatives stood in the living room waiting for the surprise, Phillip noticed that his uncles were all the same height and many of his aunts had dimples like his mom. He looked at his cousins. Many of them looked very much like him. "Dad, look at all our relatives. Many of them look alike: Why?" he asked Phillip's dad kept an eye out for his mom and replied, "Is our DNA." He checked his watch and announced, "She'II be here in a few minutes. It's time to hide." The relatives

crouched behind furniture and got quiet. "What's DNA?" whispered Phillip. "DNA is like the cake recipe we used," said Dad. "It gives cells instructions to follow as they develop. We have similar traits because we share DNA with our relatives." "Dimples come from our DNA," said Aunt Mary. "Who has them?" she asked. Several aunts, uncles, and cousins raised their hands. "Grandma Grace had dimples and so do I," said Aunt Mary, smiling."See!" Suddenly, Phillip's mom walked through the front door and asked, "What's going on in here?" "SURPRISE!" everyone yelled. "Happy birthday!" Phillip's mom rolled her eyes and smiled, showing her dimples.
