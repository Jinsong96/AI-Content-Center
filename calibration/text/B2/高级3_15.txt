Moon in the Day

One summer day, Jim and his dad were driving to a campsite in their car. "Look up at the sky!" Jim cried, pointing out the car window. "What's that big round ball?" he asked. "It's the moon!" smiled his father. "But how can that be? It's the middle of the day. We should only see the silvery moon at night, right?" Jim was puzzled. "Usually we can't see it in daylight, but the moon is always in the sky, night and day." his father answered. "Of course, when it's night time, it is much easier to see the moon." "1 only remember seeing the moon at night" said Jim. "On some days, if the sky is really clear, and the sun is not too bright, we can see the moon. Today is just like that."

Jim looked up at the sky and said, don't know much about it. Can you tell me more?" Dad thought for a minute. "Well, the moon is always travelling around our planet Earth. It takes 28 days for the moon to go around Earth one time," he explained. "It's really amazing that humans landed on the moon many years ago!" Dad nodded as he pulled into the campground. "One more thing - our moon only shows us one of its sides. There's another side of the moon that's always turned away from us. We can't see it! People call it the dark side of the moon." Then he asked, "If you had an opportunity to land on the moon, what would you say?" ''I would wave back to Earth and say, 'Hello Dad, hello people!""
