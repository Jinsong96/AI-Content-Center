A Nice Big Blanket

Planet Earth is covered in a blanket of gases. It's called our atmosphere or air, which is a collection of different gases. These gases are what we breathe in all the time. They are most precious. Air means life, not only to humans, but to all other living things, too. Within the atmosphere, some of the gases are good for us and some are not. But they are balanced and that is how life stays on Earth. A miracle, isn'tit? Most of us rarely think about the air. It's there, in us, and around us. We take it for granted. It's so natural that we breathe it in and out without any effort. Everything works the way it's supposed to. If you stop to think about it, you will be amazed by how wonderful it is that we have this atmosphere around us. This blanket of gases protects us from many harmful

things. Rocks in space coming at us burn up before they hit the ground. Ultraviolet rays that come from the sun that can hurt us are blocked by our atmosphere as well. Lucky. That's not all. The blanket also absorbs the heat from the sun during the day and retains it for the night. As a result, our nights don't get too cold. Just think about your own bed. The temperature has to be just right. If the blanket is too thick, you'll get too hot. lf it's too thin, you'll get cold. Earth needs its blanket to be just right. The way we take care of this blanket is by not polluting it. If the pollution accumulates, eventually it will damage the atmosphere, and we may lose the protection of this nice big blanket.
