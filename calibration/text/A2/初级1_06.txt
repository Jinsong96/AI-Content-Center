Many Kinds of Milk

At school, Joey's teacher says, "Milk is good for you. It helps you grow strong."

Then she asks, "Do you know where milk comes from?"

"Cows, of course!" all the children say.

"Yes, milk can come from cows. But around

the world, people also drink milk from sheep, horses, and camels," says the teacher.

Wow! Joey learns something new.

Wow! He can drink a lot of milk!
