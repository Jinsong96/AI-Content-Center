Numbers

Numbers are handy. I use them to count.

I have one nose. I have two eyes. I have three Sisters. I have four aunts and four uncles. I have five friends.

I have six hats. I have seven books.

I have eight crayons. I have nine pencils. And I have ten fingers and ten toes.

See, numbers are handy. What can you count?

I have ten

fingers!
