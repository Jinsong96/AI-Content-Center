Hats

My sister has many hats. She wears different hats when she is doing different things.

When she plays sports, she wears a baseball hat. When she works in the garden, she wears a straw hat.

When she goes to the beach, she wears a bucket hat. When she rides her bike, she wears a helmet.

It's her birthday tomorrow. Can you guess what kind of hat she will wear?

A party hat!
