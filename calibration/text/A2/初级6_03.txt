Honey

Buzz, buzz, buzz. Bees buzzing everywhere.

They look and look until they find flowers. They suck sweet water from the flowers and take it home. They use it to make honey.

What do they do with the honey? They eat it. It is their food. People like honey too. It is one of the best foods on the Earth. Do you know why? Because it never goes bad!

That's right. Even honey that is 2,000 years old is still good to eat!
