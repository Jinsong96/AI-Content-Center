A Little Reader

Karen is amazed at how much her sister Irene knows. Irene is only eight years old, but she knows lots of things. She knows about animals. She knows about trees. She even knows about dinosaurs and the stars.

Irene doesn't like to watch TV or play games. She likes to read. In her room there are books, books, and more books.

How many books has she read? About one hundred!
