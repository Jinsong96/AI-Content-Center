How Big Can It Grow?

James found a nut in the park. He asked his friend Henry, "See this nut? Do you Know how big it can become?" "I don't know," said Henry. "It can grow more than 30 metres tall." "How can that be?" asked Henry. James said, "This nut comes from an oak tree. If we plant it in the ground, it will become a big oak tree which can stretch up to the sky." "That's amazing!" said Henry. "Let's plant it now!"
