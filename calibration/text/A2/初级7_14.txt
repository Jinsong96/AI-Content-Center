Dentist Visit

Leo visited a dentist in China. He went to the dentist's office by himself. But the dentist dian 't speak English. And Leo didn't speak Chinese. "I will have to find a way to talk to the dentist," thought Leo. He sat in the dentist's chair, and opened his mouth to let the dentist see his teeth.

A few of his teeth had problems. Some needed to be pulled out. Some had to be fixed. The dentist had to talk to Leo to decide how to work on the teeth. How would they make it work? The dentist looked at Leo's teeth and talked to

him in Chinese. Leo didn't understand. They both shook their heads and smiled.

Then, the dentist found an online translator. She put the questions in Chinese. The computer changed them to English.

Then, Leo answered the questions in English. The computer gave his answers in Chinese.

The two managed to talk to each other that way. By the end of the visit, Leo got his teeth fixed.
