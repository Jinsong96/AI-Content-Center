My Dog

One day, our dog came home with a shoe in his mouth. The next day, he brought home another one, but a different kind. Where did he get the shoes? We all wondered. Just then our neighbour, Ms. Lay, came to our door. She came to warn us. There was a thief in the neighbourhood. When she washed her children's shoes and put them outside to dry, two of them were gone! My father took out the two shoes. Pointing to the dog, he said, "That's the thief!" We all laughed.

That's the thief!
