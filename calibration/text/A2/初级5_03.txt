Fire Station

Micah likes fire trucks. He is only two years old, but he knows fire trucks can do great things.

One day, he was out in a car with his grandma. When the car was passing by a fire station, Micah saw three big, shiny fire trucks.

He begged his grandma to stop. "Please! I want to see the fire trucks!" So his grandma stopped the car and they walked to the fire station. When Micah came to the fire station, a fireman was standing by the truck. He asked the man, "May l come close to see the fire truck?" "Sure!" the fireman smiled.
