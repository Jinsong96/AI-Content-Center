Too Many Pockets

My mom has a new bag. It's a Christmas gift from my dad. The new bag has many pockets. There are three big pockets and two small pockets inside the bag. There are also pockets on the outside, and one on each side of the bag.

When Mom puts her keys in the bag, it's hard for her to find the keys later. She can't remember which pocket she put them in! She says, " lost my keys inside my bag!"
