Friends

One day, Turtle lost his footing and rolled down a riverbank. He landed belly-up on his shell. The riverbank was very muddy and Turtle was stuck.

"Help! Help!" called Turtle. Cat came strolling by. "What's wrong?" asked the cat. "I'm stuck. Can you roll me over?" asked Turtle. Cat pushed Turtle over onto his belly. Turtle swam away and said, "Thanks Cat. I'll remember this!"

Not long after, Cat was chasing a squirrel on a tree. Snap! The tree branch cracked and fell. Cat landed on a large rock in the middle of the river.

"Help! Help!" called Cat. Turtle came swimming by. "What's wrong?" asked Turtle. " can't swim. Can you carry me to land?" asked Cat.

"'Sure." said Turtle. "Climb onto my back."

Cat rode to safety on the back of her new friend, Turtle.
