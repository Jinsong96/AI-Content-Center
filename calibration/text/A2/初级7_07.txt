Names

A name can tell us a lot about something. An animal named the anteater loves to eat ants. A little insect named the honeybee is a bee and it loves to eat honey. There is a bird named the red-winged blackbird. Can you guess what this bird looks like? Yes, it is a black bird with red wings. Also, a walking stick is not a stick. It is an insect, but it really looks like a stick.

A stick can't walk. But a walking stick can.
