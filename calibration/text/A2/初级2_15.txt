School Bus

Karen likes the big yellow school bus. When she sees her sister get on it every morning, she wants to ride the bus too.

But her mom says, "Karen, you are too young. You need to eat more, sleep more and play more. Then you will grow big enough to ride that big school bus." From then on, every day, Karen asks her mom, "Am l big enough to go to school now?" Her mom says, "Not yet."

One day, Karen's mom says, "Karen, you are a big girl now. It's time for you to go to school.

lomorrow, you can ride the big yellow school bus with your sister. Are you ready?"

"Yes!" Karen is very excited. Her dream to ride the big yellow school bus will come true!
