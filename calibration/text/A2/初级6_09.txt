Uncle's Phone

Uncle Anthony loves his phone. He takes it everywhere he goes.

Even at home, he always has his phone nearby. He wants to be able to check his emails often.

One day, on a business trip, his phone was stolen! Uncle Anthony was very upset. He felt lost without his phone. He didn't know how he was going to keep up with his work. "It's the worst day of my life," said my uncle.

Uncle Anthony used his phone for many things. He used it to take pictures. Then, all of his pictures were gone. He used the phone as a

watch. Without the phone, he could not see what time it was. That was not all. He used his phone to send and receive emails. But on that day, he had no way to check his email. Of course, he could not talk to his family on the phone either. "'Suddenly, I am thrown back to 30 years ago!" said my poor uncle.
