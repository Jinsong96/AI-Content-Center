Pumpkins for Sale

October 31st was coming soon. While my mom was driving home, she saw many pumpkins on the road side.

No one was there, just a sign. It said: Pumpkins for sale. Large $10. Small $5.

My mom stopped the car. She took two large pumpkins. As she put the money in the basket, she saw many twenty-dollar bills inside. "Isn't it nice that people can still trust each other like this?" Mom said to me.

Nice to see people can trust each other
