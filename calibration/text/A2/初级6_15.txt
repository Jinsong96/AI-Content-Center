The Same but Different

Becky and Sherry are twins. Becky loves dresses. Her sister Sherry loves sports. One day, their mother said to them, "Let's buy some new clothes for you." So they went to the store together. Their mother chose some very nice dresses for them. Becky was happy. But Sherry was not. "How can l play baseball in this?" she asked.

Sherry looked at a boy who was trying on a pair of shorts.

Sherry$ mom saw this. "Well," she said. "Would you like some shorts instead?"

Sherry was very happy. "That would be great!" she said.

"All right. I will buy a dress for Becky and shorts for Sherry."Mom said. "Sometimes forget that although you two look the same, it doesn't mean you ARE the same," laughed their mother.
