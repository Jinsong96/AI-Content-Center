Who Runs Faster?

If you had many legs, do you think you could run faster? Well, let's think about it. A centipede may have one hundred legs. A millipede may have as many as one thousand legs. A spider has only eight legs. But a spider runs faster than a centipede. It even runs faster than a millipede.

You see, having many legs doesn't make you a fast runner.

This spider is a fast runner!
