Go Under the Water

I can swim. But I am afraid of putting my head under the water. My brother is a good swimmer. He is teaching me.

"Take a deep breath of air, and hold it," he says. "Now put your head under the water." I take a deep breath. I hold it, and l put my head under the water. I'm doing it! I'm under the water.

I can open my eyes. I see lots of little fish. l don't feel afraid. I have a great teacher!

Hold your breath, then go!
