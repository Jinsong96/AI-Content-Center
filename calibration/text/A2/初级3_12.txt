Our Friendly Star

The Sun is our closest star in the sky. It is very strong and powerful.

What does the Sun do for us? Well, it keeps the Earth warm. It makes the plants grow. We can even use the Sun to help us turn the lights on in our homes. This is called solar Power. The Sun is our friend. If there were no Sun, we could not live here on planet Earth.
