Baby in the Car!

Baby Eddy likes riding in the car. But he doesn't like the seat belt. He cries when his mother puts the seat belt on him.

His mom says, "The car moves very fast. If you do not wear the belt, you may fly out of your car seat and get hurt."

Then, she gives the baby a toy. He stops crying.

"You're too young to understand car safety, but you're not too young to get hurt" says his mom.

Play with your toy, Eddy.
