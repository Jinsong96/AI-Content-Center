The Big Tree

There is a big tree in front of my house. It's a beautiful tree full of green leaves.

The weather is dry and hot this summer. Every day, my mom asks me to drink a lot of water.

One day, l asked my mom, "Does the tree need to drink water too?"

Mom said, "Yes, of course. Every living thing needs water. Trees too."

Then she said, "If you water the tree on these hot days, the tree will be really happy." The big tree is my friend. It gives me shade and more. I shall do something tor it.

I took a bucket, got water from inside of the house and poured the water on the tree's roots.

One bucket, two buckets with ten full buckets of water!

"Now that you have had so much watek. you will not be thirsty any more!" I said to the tree
