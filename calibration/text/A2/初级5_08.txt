Joe's Sweet Tooth

Joe has a sweet tooth. That means he likes to eat sweets. But Joe doesn't like to brush his teeth.

His mother says, "If you don't brush your teeth, soon you're going to get a toothache." Joe says, "If my teeth hurt me, I will have them pulled out." His mother laughs. "It's not that easy." she says. "Would you like to know what happens if you pull out all of your teeth?" "Yes!" Joe is very interested. "Everyone only gets two sets of teeth. First, we

get baoy teeth. At age 6, our baby teeth fall out, and we grow new strong teeth, " says his mother.

"And then what?" asks Joe.

"After that, we don't grow new teeth any more. So, if you get the teeth pulled out, you won't be able to eat all the delicious food that you like," says his mother. "Oh no!" Joe now knows how bad things can be if he does not take care of his teeth.
