Clean the Beach

Hal was playing on the beach with his friends. Ouch! His foot was cut. He called out to the lifeguard for help.

" will put a bandage on your cut." said the lifeguard "Why would someone leave glass on the beach?" asked one of Hal's friends.

"Let's do something about it," said the lifeguard. "Let's clean the beach!" So, they walked along the beach and picked up the garbage.

A clean beach is a safe beach!
