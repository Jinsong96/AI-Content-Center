Wash Your Hands

"It's time for lunch," the teacher tells her class. "Don't forget. There is something you have to do first." What is it? They need to wash their hands.

"When you wash your hands, don't do it quickly. As you wash, sing the alphabet song. When you're done, your hands will be really clean," says the teacher

"A, B, C, D, E, F, G." The students sing the song as they wash their hands. Now their hands are clean and it's time to eat!
