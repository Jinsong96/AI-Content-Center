Amy Gets a Bike

Amy wants a bike. She wants a yellow bike. One day, her dad says, "Lets get you a bike today." They go to the bike store. The new bike is expensive. It costs more than a hundred dollars. The bike store also sells used bikes. The used bikes are good bikes too. They cost only fifty dollars and there is a yellow one.

Looking at the price, Amy says, " will take the used bike." Why? Amy knows that money does not grow on trees.
