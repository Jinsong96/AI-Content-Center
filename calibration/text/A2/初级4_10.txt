My Mouth

Open wide. Smile. Eat. Talk to my friends. What do l use to do all these things? My mouth, of course.

Look in my mouth. What do you see? Teeth. Yes, my teeth help me chew my food so l can eat.

What else do you see? My tongue. My tongue helps me talk. It also helps me move food around, so l can swallow it.
