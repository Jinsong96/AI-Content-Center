Learning to Fish

There was a young mother bear who would bring a fish to her baby bear every day. One day, a wise old bear said to the young mother, "If you bring your baby a fish, he won't be hungry for the rest of the day. But if you teach him how to fish, he won't be hungry for the rest of his life."

The young mother bear thought about it. She knew what she would do.

Teach him how to fish.
