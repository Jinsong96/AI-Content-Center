The Days of the Week

Jill is learning the days of the week.

"Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday, " she chants with her dad on the way to daycare.

Dad tells her that Monday to Friday are week days. Children go to school. Parents go to work. But Saturday and Sunday are the weekend days.

At the end of the week, it's time to rest and play. Hooray! The weekend is on the way. MON
