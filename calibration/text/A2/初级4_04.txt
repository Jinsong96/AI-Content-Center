Celebrating Children's Day

Mr. Lee is 82 years old. He retired from teaching many years ago. But his students still remember him. They often come to visit him on holidays. A few days ago, he sent a message to his students. He was leaving to visit his mother in his hometown.

How old is his mother? One hundred and three. Yes, she is 103 years old. Mr. Lee said that he would celebrate a special day with his mother on June 1st - that's International Children's Day! His students were amazed.

Happy Children's Day!
