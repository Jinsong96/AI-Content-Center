A Green Christmas

Christmas Day is always on December 25th. It's usually very cold at that time of the year. There is usually a lot of snow on the ground. When it snows on Christmas Day, people call it a white Christmas.

But this Christmas day is unusual. It's not cold at all. The weather is warm, and there is no snow because it's 18°C outside.

I guess people could call this a green Christmas!

Merry Christmas!
