How Did He Know?

Uncle Bob is a bus driver. One day, while he was driving to work, his car stopped running. His car was on a busy street. So he ran to the gas station for help. He wanted to run fast, but he couldn't. For the first time in his life, he felt that his body was very heavy. That was not the body he remembered. Just a few years ago, he was able to jump high and run fast, and he was never out of breath.

Uncle Bob said, "On that day, I knew that was t0o heavy. I need to do exercise to keep in shape."

I can't believe myself!
