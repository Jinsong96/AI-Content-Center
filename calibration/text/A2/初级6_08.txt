Which One First?

"If I drop an apple and a watermelon from the same height, which one will hit the ground first?" asks Tom.

"That's so easy!" says Mary. "The watermelon is big and heavy. It will hit the ground first." Tom smiles. He knows what will happen. He says, "No, they will hit the ground at the same time."

"What? No way!" Mary doesn't believe it.

"It's true. Thats science," Tom says. Try it yourself!
