Cat Adventure

My mom was worried. She had not seen our cat all day, but she could hear her meowing. We looked all over the house. We looked in the kitchen, in the bedroom, and even in the Dathroom.

The whole time we could hear her meowing, but the cat wasn't anywhere in the house.

Finally, I looked outside and there she was. Our cat was on the roof!!

The cat went up to the top of our house! Someone left the window open and our cat jumped outside and onto the roof.

It was too high for her to jump back down. Finally, my mom dlimbed up and carried the cat down.

She said, "You should think about coming down before you climb up!"

There she is!
